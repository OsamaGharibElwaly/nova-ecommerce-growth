# NOVA 30-Day E-commerce Growth Strategy

## Executive objective
Improve the performance of a fictional Egyptian fashion e-commerce store across acquisition, conversion, and retention. This is a planning exercise using synthetic assumptions, not a live campaign.

## Strategic priorities
1. Improve product discovery with platform-specific content.
2. Increase qualified traffic rather than optimizing for reach alone.
3. Improve product detail pages and reduce checkout friction.
4. Recover high-intent sessions through consent-based lifecycle messaging.
5. Increase repeat purchases through relevant product recommendations.

## Campaign phases
| Period | Focus | Core activity | Primary KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-7 | Awareness | Brand story, product education, creator-style videos | Reach, video views |
| Days 8-14 | Consideration | Product comparisons, reviews, styling content | Product views, CTR |
| Days 15-21 | Conversion | Best sellers, bundles, retargeting | Orders, CPA, ROAS |
| Days 22-30 | Retention | Post-purchase content, cross-sell, feedback | Repeat purchase rate |

## Channel roles
- Meta: prospecting, catalog/product promotion, and retargeting.
- TikTok: discovery and short-form creative testing.
- Google Search: capture users expressing product/category intent.
- Email / WhatsApp: customer service and lifecycle communication, subject to consent and applicable rules.

## Audience hypotheses
- Trend seekers (18-24): discover products through short-form video and creator content.
- Value seekers (25-34): compare price, quality, delivery, and return policy.
- Existing customers: respond to relevant new arrivals and complementary products.

## Budget hypothesis
| Channel | Budget (EGP) | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Meta | 16,000 | 40% |
| TikTok | 10,000 | 25% |
| Google | 8,000 | 20% |
| Testing / retargeting reserve | 6,000 | 15% |
| Total | 40,000 | 100% |

Budget is a scenario for planning. Reallocate only after reviewing actual performance, margin, stock, and tracking quality.

## Success criteria
Use contribution margin and incremental business outcomes alongside platform metrics. Avoid treating impressions, clicks, or platform-reported ROAS as business results without validation.
