# Paid Media Plan

## Planning allocation
| Channel | Budget (EGP) | Role | Audience | Creative | Primary KPI |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | 16,000 | Prospecting + retargeting | New and engaged audiences | Product carousel, short video | CPA, ROAS |
| TikTok | 10,000 | Discovery | Trend and style interests | Short-form video | Qualified visits, CTR |
| Google Search | 8,000 | Intent capture | Category / product searchers | Search ads | Conversion rate, CPA |
| Test reserve | 6,000 | Controlled tests | Best-performing segments | Creative and audience tests | Incremental efficiency |

## Testing plan
- Test two creative hooks per product category.
- Change one major variable at a time where practical.
- Set minimum observation windows before making decisions.
- Check stock, margin, landing page, and tracking before scaling.
- Report platform-reported attribution separately from store orders.

## Guardrails
Do not promise a ROAS or sales outcome before live data exists. Ensure ad copy and targeting comply with platform policies and local law.
