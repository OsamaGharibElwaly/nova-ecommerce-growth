# CRM & Retention Playbook

Use customer communications only where the customer has provided appropriate consent and the channel's rules are met.

## Lifecycle segments
- New: first order or newly registered customer.
- Returning: more than one completed order.
- Loyal: repeated purchasing over time.
- At-risk: no purchase within a defined period; determine threshold from actual buying cycles.

## Message 1 — Welcome
Subject: Welcome to NOVA

Thanks for discovering NOVA. Explore everyday essentials, check product details and sizing, and contact our team if you need help choosing.

## Message 2 — Cart reminder
Subject: Still considering your NOVA pick?

You left an item in your cart. You can return to review the product details, delivery information, and available sizes whenever you're ready.

## Message 3 — Post-purchase
Subject: Thanks for your order

Thank you for shopping with NOVA. We hope you enjoy your purchase. If you need help with care, sizing, or your order, our support team is here to help.

## Retention experiments
- Relevant complementary product recommendations.
- Back-in-stock notifications for opted-in customers.
- Post-purchase feedback request.
- Loyalty benefit test with margin controls.
