# Creative Brief — NOVA Everyday Essentials

## Objective
Create a consistent set of assets that introduce NOVA and encourage qualified product-page visits.

## Audience
Fashion shoppers aged 18-34 in Egypt. This is a planning hypothesis, not a validated demographic profile.

## Message
Everyday essentials that are easy to style.

## Deliverables
- 1 brand introduction post
- 1 product carousel
- 1 vertical short-form video
- 2 story frames

## Visual direction
Modern, clean, product-first photography. Keep product details and pricing accurate. Use readable typography and a consistent color system.

## Required checks
- Confirm product, price, stock, and delivery details.
- Check mobile readability.
- Include a clear CTA.
- Do not use unlicensed images or fabricate customer testimonials.
