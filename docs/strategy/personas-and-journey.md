# Customer Personas & Journey

These personas are hypotheses for a fictional brand. Validate them with interviews, analytics, surveys, and customer service data before making real budget decisions.

## Persona 1 — Trend Seeker
- Age band: 18-24
- Need: discover affordable styles and outfit ideas
- Likely discovery: TikTok and Instagram
- Barriers: uncertain sizing, price, and product quality
- Content: styling reels, new arrivals, user-generated-content concepts
- CTA: Explore the collection

## Persona 2 — Value Seeker
- Age band: 25-34
- Need: reliable everyday products and clear value
- Likely discovery: search, Facebook, Instagram
- Barriers: delivery cost, return policy, trust, and product details
- Content: product comparisons, quality details, bundles
- CTA: View product details

## Customer journey
| Stage | Customer question | Touchpoint | Opportunity | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | What is NOVA? | Social / search | Clear brand promise | Reach |
| Consideration | Is this right for me? | Product page / reviews | Better product information | Product view rate |
| Conversion | Can I trust this purchase? | Cart / checkout | Transparent delivery and returns | Conversion rate |
| Retention | What should I buy next? | Email / WhatsApp | Relevant recommendations | Repeat purchase rate |
