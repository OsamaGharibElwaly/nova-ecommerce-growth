## Research questions
1. Which product categories receive the most visibility?
2. How are discounts and bundles communicated?
3. What information appears above the fold on product pages?
4. How clear are shipping, payment, and return policies?
5. What formats are used on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok?
6. What customer objections appear in public reviews?

Do not invent competitor metrics. If a metric is unavailable, record "Not publicly available".
